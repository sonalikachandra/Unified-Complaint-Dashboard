Uniﬁed Customer Complaint Communication Dashboard
AI-powered complaint intelligence platform that collects complaints from 
multiple channels (such as email, branch logs, chat, call center notes, and 
social media),  then automatically:
● Categorizes complaints,
● Detects sentiment and urgency,
● Identifies duplicate or repeated issues,
● Generates suggested responses,
● Tracks SLA and escalation,
● Shows trends by branch, product, region, and complaint type.
